### B1: one spelling for the reading direction

The UI stores `rtl` / `ltr` / `ttb`. Older series, `scripts/playwright/*` and the batch harness
store `rightToLeft` / `leftToRight`. The backend sends the value lowercased (`righttoleft`), and
the worker compared against `"rtl"` in `merge_ocr_regions` and against `!= "ltr"` in panel
detection and the grouping binding check. So:
- a `rightToLeft` series read every joined region's columns left to right;
- a `leftToRight` series (ko/zh from the scripts) got manga panel order and the vertical binding.

Ten of the laptop's thirteen series are `rightToLeft`. Fix: `normalize_reading_direction` in
the worker, called where the OCR and panel handlers read the job. Same spellings the frontend's
`utils/readingDirection.ts` accepts. A series stored as `rtl` / `ltr` / `ttb` sees no change.

Gate: sample78, sample27 and sample118 read in the right order on the chrome-box re-run.
**Passed 2026-10-06** (chrome-box, *Redo OCR* on all 22 ja pages with worker commit `dacd7e8`, the head of worker #55 (B2), which sits on `9619505` (B1, this
PR's pin); the image was built from that commit's checkout):
21 of 22 pages keep exactly the same region boxes as on `1dfe23e`; only joined text changes, now in
reading order (「放課後デートしようよ」, 「ここでサインですよね」, 「私の家にこの先ずっと一緒に住むというのは
どうだろう？」). Still wrong both before and after: ja/sample4, horizontal Chinese on a right-to-left
series (goes to B4).

### B2: the gutter of a two-page spread

`find_spread_gutter` looks for a vertical edge within 8 % of the centre that covers 85 % of the
height of a page at least 1.2 times as wide as tall. Text that no balloon holds is then grouped
per page. If a piece of text lies across the seam with 35 % or more of its width on each side, the
seam is art, not a binding, and nothing changes. sample93's left-page shout balloon is drawn over
the binding with 22 % on the far side, so it stays with its page.

Evidence:
- 40 wide corpus pages: fires on the two real spreads only (sample93 0.96, sample113 0.89). The
  highest single illustration is sample92 (a checked wall) at 0.81.
- Pixiv folders, images 1.25–1.6 wide: a random 36 of the hits were all two pages or two panels
  side by side, with the line on the boundary.
- Replayed on sample93's fragments: left page one region, right page's speech one region, its
  aside 「えっと…なんでしたっけ…あっ」 its own. Nothing crosses the gutter.

Not fixed by B2: on sample93's left page a shout, a sound effect and a balloon still join (B5).

Gate: sample93 has no region across x≈3500 on a re-run; sample113 likewise; the other 38 wide
corpus pages are unchanged.
**sample93 passed 2026-10-06** (same re-run): the one region at x 2186–4643 became the left page
(x 2186–3633), the right page's speech (x 3646–4432, 「あ、悪の怪人センせーデスさん私…の…っ
ま、マジカル☆ロイヤルおまんこぉ～…っ…で！退治させていただき…」) and its aside
(「えっと…なんでしたっけ…あっ」). The guard logged on page 22 only. sample113 and the other wide
pages are not in that chapter; their detector numbers are above.

### B3: the balloons #230 still splits (#175's remaining positives, #231 item 1)

**The cause is not measured yet, and it is probably not one cause.** The boxes from the `1dfe23e`
run do not fit "a gap a little over 0.35 characters" for all of them:
- sample27 panel 1: オタクくん ends at x 749+37, the joined 放課後デートしようよ ends at 632+74 = 706, so
  the gap is about 43 px against ~37 px columns, about 1.2 characters. A 0.8 budget would not join
  it.
- sample78: 男子ぃ (x 1114–1162) overlaps the joined region (x 1125–1202) sideways. That is not a gap;
  something else keeps them apart.
- sample258: the region scored here (`bubble_0`, two sentences) may not be the balloon the gate
  means ("round, 3 columns of unequal length"); score against the survey sheet's boxes.

So step 1 is a table, one row per miss, from the chrome-box captures (`OCR_CAPTURE_DIR` is set on
that stack) replayed with the worker's own `ocr_capture.py`: gap in characters, which gate removed
the edge (proximity, waist, owner veto and its reason), whether the pieces share a validated
balloon. Step 2 fixes the causes the table shows, like #53 did.

For #231's paragraph-gap pages (sample25 about 0.54, sample27 panel 3 about 0.46, zh/sample200
about 0.42) the candidate is a second, balloon-level pass: two components inside **one validated
balloon** join when the gap is under a larger in-balloon budget (start at 0.8), their lines run
side by side, the waist veto finds no outline between them (so fused balloons such as sample174,
218, 3, 30, 24 and ko/sample182 stay apart), and the joined group passes the owner check. A bigger
budget everywhere is not safe: at 0.5 it made 2 false merges on the 2026-08-09 hand labels, and
from 0.6 TELEA p. 5's 怎么样 aside dragged its balloon apart.

Either change is measured offline first (captures, the 2026-08-09 hand labels, the guard pages)
and ships on by default only if no guard page changes and the hand labels show no new false
merge, as #53 did.

## Not B, found on the way

- **Translation model.** The test chapter pinned `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash`. OpenRouter's
  cheapest-first routing sent it to the OpenInference host, which ignored our 4,096-token
  reasoning budget: 8,192 tokens of reasoning, `finish=length`, no translation, about 220 s and
  $0.012 a call, then the fallback (`v4-pro`, 83 s). This is the "DeepSeek has fallen off"
  symptom. Separate PR off `main`: new defaults, and `require_parameters` so a host that does not
  honour the request is skipped.
- **`handlers/layout.py` hardcodes `reading_direction = "rtl"`,** so left-to-right comics get
  right-to-left bubble order. Not B; one line for the follow-up list.
- **The empty translation of an aside** (#231 item 3) is the translation stage; it moves to that
  PR's test pages.
