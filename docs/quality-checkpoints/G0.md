## Open uncertainties carried forward

- The six originals' snapshots carry no rendered font size, so the 24 px readability floor is evidenced only by the conventional controls.
- 34 reviewed holdout regions remain unresolved `review` cases: 15 boxes spanning several speech containers and 19 detector regions with no text. They may not be silently replaced by any later task.
- Reserve-page labels are unreviewed drafts by design; a reserve page needs source-pixel label review before it may replace a roster page.
- Raw A02 oriented fragments are retained for only two control pages, so fragment membership is unverifiable elsewhere.
- `px-res-1` (`sample656`) has provisional language metadata: its dominant script was not confirmed by visual review and must be verified from its OCR output before use.
- QA status is a pipeline signal, not a quality pass. No page in this gate is claimed to have acceptable output quality.
