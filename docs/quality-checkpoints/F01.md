# F01: conservative fragment-owner decisions

Status: **DONE**.

## Boundary

`worker/src/worker/services/owner_assignment.py` is pure. It receives the captured oriented quads, candidate components, detector polygons, recognition/region source-style declarations, and OCR-to-source scale. It emits one `assigned` or explicit `unknown` decision per candidate component.

A multi-fragment owner is assigned only when all fragments are inside exactly one validated detector polygon, declare the same source style, and form coherent oriented lines with bounded scale-normalized separation and lateral continuity. A malformed quad, scale, polygon, missing style, mismatched style, discontinuity, or different containers is an `unknown` decision with a machine-readable reason. Single fragments remain their own bounded owners.

Panels, conversations, bounding-box overlap, and proximity are absent from this decision surface. They cannot establish a cleanup/text owner.

`worker/src/worker/services/ocr_capture.py` is the narrow adapter. New captures are `ocr-grouping-capture-v2` and retain the frozen observed groups in `final_owners` for comparison while adding `owner_decisions`. Its new evidence does not change `fragment_grouping.py`, `merge_regions.py`, or live OCR output; F02–F04 consume this decision surface for component vetoes and runtime cutover.

The tests prove: bounded assignment for continuous lines in one validated styled container; same panel/conversation alone cannot assign; overlapping boxes without style stay unknown; separate validated containers veto the candidate; discontinuity stays unknown; singleton ownership is explicit; and malformed candidate partitions fail.

## Captured evidence

[Machine-readable evidence](../quality-runs/f01-20260916-owner-assignment/evidence.json) evaluates both retained A06-C fragment captures without changing their frozen v1 files. Of 37 fragments and 18 legacy candidate components, the new decision boundary assigns only seven singleton owners and leaves 11 multi-fragment candidates / 30 fragments explicitly unknown: nine candidates lack source-style evidence and two lack validated containers. This is the intended conservative result, not a quality pass or a claim that those legacy components are wrong.
