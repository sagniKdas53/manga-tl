# F01: conservative fragment-owner decisions

Status: **DONE**.

## Boundary

`worker/src/worker/services/owner_assignment.py` is pure. It receives captured oriented quads, candidate components, detector polygons, recognition/region source-style declarations, and OCR-to-source scale. It emits one `assigned` or explicit `unknown` decision per candidate component.

A multi-fragment owner requires an unambiguous geometrically valid shared detector container, finite source scale, and coherent oriented lines with bounded scale-normalized separation and lateral continuity. Available declared source style is supporting evidence: contradictory declarations veto; matching style never establishes an owner alone; missing or partial style is recorded as unknown and does not force a split. A malformed quad, scale, missing/ambiguous container, incompatible declared styles, or discontinuity yields an `unknown` decision with a machine-readable reason. Single fragments remain their own bounded owners.

Panels, conversations, bounding-box overlap, and proximity are absent from this decision surface. They cannot establish a cleanup/text owner.

`worker/src/worker/services/ocr_capture.py` remains the narrow adapter. New captures are `ocr-grouping-capture-v2`, retain the frozen observed groups in `final_owners` for comparison, and add `owner_decisions`. This changes neither `fragment_grouping.py`, `merge_regions.py`, nor live OCR output; F02–F04 consume the decision surface for component vetoes and runtime cutover.
