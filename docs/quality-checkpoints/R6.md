## What it was

Not the container. The "72×176 container for a 150 px balloon" in the R2 report is the polygon the
worker persists *after* the split (`ownershipProvenance.containerResolution = resolved-local-split`,
`handlers/ocr.py:1018`: `get_split_polygon` clips the bubble mask to the fragment's bbox ± 20).
Stitching the five local crops of sample641's `bubble_3` back together gives the full ~190×250
balloon, with a rounded upper-right corner running from (551,103) to (579,149) — and the first
column's top-right corner at (560,124) sits just past that curve.

That is the whole mechanism, on all three pages: **a balloon is an ellipse and a text column is a
rectangle.** The outermost column (ja/zh) or the top and bottom line (ko) of any multi-line balloon
has corners outside the curve, and F01's containment test (`_containing_container`) asked for all
four corners inside. So the veto declined exactly the balloons that hold multi-column sentences —
`sample7 bubble_2` had *both* columns outside, `sample197 bubble_9` all three lines. Option (b)
(merge the insiders) cannot fix those; option (c) has nothing to fix.

Measured, because each fragment's local-split polygon is the mask clipped to a window that contains
its own quad, testing a quad against its own local polygon is an exact point-in-mask test. Fraction
of quad area inside the balloon (16×16 grid) for the 42 balloon fragments on the three pages:

| fraction inside | fragments | what they are |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1.000 | 28 | interior columns/lines, and sample641's "missing" column (a sub-2 px corner miss) |
| 0.95–0.999 | 9 | edge columns of ja balloons |
| 0.875–0.941 | 4 | the top/bottom lines of the ko balloons `bubble_8`/`bubble_9` (sample197) |
| 0.618 | 1 | `追加？` (sample7 `bubble_5`): a rotated quad in a group that already fails `insufficient-lateral-line-overlap` — not R20's case |

Every fragment that belongs to its balloon is ≥ 0.875 inside. The floor is **0.75** — margin below
the real minimum, and a quad half outside still fails.

## What changed (worker `1c62e13`)

`services/owner_assignment.py` only:

- `_containing_container` returns the one container holding ≥ `_MIN_QUAD_INSIDE_FRACTION` (0.75)
  of the quad, plus the best fraction seen; two containers claiming one quad is still an ambiguity
  (`None`). New `_quad_inside_fraction` samples a 16×16 bilinear grid over the quad (the mask can
  be concave, so it is sampled rather than clipped; pure Python, ~256 point tests per quad).
- `assign_captured_owners` records `container_coverage` (per-member fraction) next to
  `container_ids` in the diagnostics — gate item 3 reads it.
- Unchanged on purpose: `different-validated-containers` (never bridge two balloons), the
  two-member `geometry-attached-continuous-lines` path (the existing tests put that outsider at 0
  and 0.5 inside, so they land on the same path), `_line_continuity`, the live veto in
  `handlers/ocr.py`.
- Tests: `test_assigns_columns_whose_corners_poke_past_an_elliptical_balloon` (five columns in an
  ellipse, corners provably outside → one owner) and
  `test_a_column_mostly_outside_the_balloon_is_still_not_contained` (adjacent column straddling
  the edge at 0.48 → `incomplete-validated-container`). Gates: ruff clean, pyright at its baseline
  (three pre-existing `test_catalog_refresh.py` errors), **515 passed**.

## Where it is (as filed; superseded by the section above)

All in the worker (`manga-tl-worker` index):

- `services/owner_assignment.py` `assign_captured_owners` — a multi-fragment owner needs every member's quad with **all four corners** inside **one** validated container (`_containing_container`, `_point_in_polygon`). Line continuity (`_line_continuity`) passed on nearly every balloon in the run; the decline is `incomplete-validated-container` / `missing-validated-container`. The only partial-merge path is two members with one outsider (`owner_assignment.py:142`).
- `handlers/ocr.py` `owner_aware_grouping_context` / `attach_live_owner_decisions` — F04's live veto: an `unknown` decision splits the component to singletons.
- The detector container: the recorded container for sample641's `bubble_3` is 72×176 for a 150 px-wide balloon, i.e. column-sized. Whether that is YOLO's box, the contour fallback, or the mask-to-polygon step needs one look (`bubble_detector`, the contour fallback in `handlers/ocr.py`) before choosing between fixing the test and fixing the container.

Candidate fixes, to be decided with the impact report: (a) containment by quad centre or by ≥ N % of quad area instead of all four corners; (b) merge the members that share one container, emit only the outsiders as singletons; (c) fix the container. The rule's own docstring ("Panels, conversations, bounding-box overlap, and proximity are deliberately not inputs") is the constraint: (a)/(b) stay inside it, and neither must bridge two containers.
